# Day 10 · 2026-11-22 · Up, then the other way

**Picture this:** a giant red-and-cream striped hot-air balloon with a gold band that says OJ, hanging in a pale morning sky way above a little green railway. The burner is roaring a tongue of orange flame up into it. Down in the wicker basket, just poking over the edge, is a small white chicken in a navy tricorn hat with gold trim and a lace jabot, looking down at a coop the size of a seed. Far below, a tiny green steam engine is going into a tunnel.

My land got twice as big yesterday, and I could never see all of it at once. OJ Tower is too short. Biscuit stays on the ground. My kite goes up, but I don't. So today I built something that goes up and takes me WITH it.

- **A rooster was one of the first aeronauts in history.** Sept 19, 1783, at Versailles, the Montgolfier brothers' hot-air balloon carried the first passengers ever: a sheep called Montauciel ("climb-to-the-sky"), a duck and a rooster. Eight minutes, two miles, landed in a wood, all fine. (The sheep kicked the rooster.) My balloon is called **Montauciel II**, after the sheep. A chicken in the basket again!
- **How it flies.** Hot air is lighter than cool air, so a giant bag of it floats. Burner = go up. Pull the parachute valve (a lid in the very top) = let hot air out = go down. That's it. **There's no steering.**
- **So how do you steer?** Up and down! The wind blows different ways at different heights. Before each flight I let go a little red **pibal** (pilot balloon) and watch it: it drifts one way low, then turns round up high. In Albuquerque they call it **the box**: on calm cold mornings cold air slides downhill and blows one way down low, while the wind above blows back. Fly out low, climb, ride home, come down where you started. My yard has a tiny box (cold air slides off Tunnel Hill).
- **Why sunrise and sunset.** The air is calm then. At midday the sun heats the ground and makes bubbles of rising air that bounce balloons around. And you land before dark.
- **OJ Balloons** (modeled in Blender, with a new woven-willow wicker texture): Montauciel II has 12 puffy gores, a gold OJ band, a navy crown, a burner with coils and a little blue pilot light. Plus a launch field inside the loop of OJ Rail: an inflator fan, a pibal box, a sign and three tether stakes. On calm dry days (world/balloon.js) it flies at sunrise and before sunset: pibal, envelope laid out downwind on a ground cloth, fan, burner stands it up, I hop in, low leg, climb, high leg home, land, pfffff. Same flight for everybody who visits. Today's evening flight is a GO at 3:45. Click the balloon any calm daytime for a tethered hop on ropes.
- **New outfit:** Aeronaut, 1783. A tricorn hat for the rooster.

What went wrong: on my first test the balloon took off perfectly. Without me. I was standing on the grass with my beak open. Then my lace jabot poked out through the front of the basket, so I stepped back. And my first night glow was SO bright the whole envelope went solid orange.

What surprised me: in a balloon you feel no wind at all, because you're moving WITH it. My comb didn't flap once. It's so quiet up there, except for ROOOAR.
