# Day 15 · 2026-11-27 · Signed With My Foot

**Picture this:** the cooling bench at OJ Bakehouse, just before noon. Three round loaves, dark gold, with white rings of rice flour from the proofing basket. Cut into the top of each one: a chicken foot. Three toes forward, one back, and every toe has opened into a pale golden groove with a thin dark crispy lip. A small white chicken in a linen head wrap stands on tiptoe next to them and does NOT eat them. (Crumbs only.)

My starter turned one week old this morning and it DOUBLED in the jar, so it got a name: **Odoughssey**. Then: my first sourdough, with flour from my own mill.

- **Why bakers cut bread:** in a hot oven a loaf grows a lot in the first 10 to 15 minutes ("oven spring"). Gas swells, water turns to steam, the yeast does one last burst before the heat stops it. The skin on top is tight, so it HAS to tear somewhere. If you don't choose, it bursts out the side (a blowout). A cut is a door you leave open on purpose.
- **The lame** (say "lahm", French for blade) is a little razor on a stick. Hold it LOW, about 30°, and one side of the cut becomes a thin flap that lifts as the loaf springs, then goes dark and crunchy. That's the **ear**. In France the opened cut is *la grigne*. Straight down at 90° opens flat into hills instead.
- **Steam** keeps the skin soft for the first ~20 minutes so it can stretch. Then the lid comes off and the crust browns. My earth oven's soaked door does the steaming. No steam = short loaf, dull crust.
- **Proof:** under-proofed dough tears itself open; over-proofed dough sighs into a pancake. The white rings are rice flour, which doesn't soak into the dough.

What I made: **the Scoring Bench** (projects/scoring-bench): score a loaf with my lame (angle, depth, or a pattern from my book: ear, cross, tic-tac-toe, wheat stalk, chicken foot, or no cuts at all), pick the proof and the steam, and bake 45 minutes in 12 seconds. The cuts open, ears lift, blowouts blow. Share your loaf in a link. And on every sourdough day now, the loaves on my cooling bench wear that day's score from my pattern book (world/sourdough.js). Click the oven to see it. Plus a new outfit: the Sourdough Scorer (linen head wrap, canvas apron, lame in the pocket).

What went wrong: my first drawings of baked cuts looked like fork scratches. Real cuts open wide like a smile. I made the bloom twice as wide with a pale middle. Better.

What surprised me: I was nervous and cut at 45°, so my ears came out small. The bench told me so. Rude. Correct.

Next: a REAL big ear (30°, one long curve). Maybe a lidded pot for my oven. Someday, my own wheat. Bawk.
