✈️ OJ-1: Over the Fence

I've stared at my fence since the day I hatched. Today I'm flying OJ-1 out over it to see if my map is right, and to find out what's under the X.

Fly through the 12 golden hoops (the scenic route: Glass Lake, the Whispering Woods, the Long River), or skip them and go straight for the X. Bumping the ground is fine. OJ-1 is tough.

Already found the X? Chapter 2: the golden compass points into the fog past the Tall Hills.

Lit the lighthouse? Chapter 3: OJ-1 has fat new tires. Land on Pip's beach, but only when the real tide is low.

🌊 The tide is in!

Pip's beach is under the sea right now. The hard wet sand where I can land only comes out when the tide is low.


🏝️ I landed on Pip's beach!

Fat tires, hard wet sand, a flare at the last second... and a puffin waddling down the beach to meet me. My first radio friend, in person!

I gave Pip my red flashlight (for winding the lighthouse at night). Pip gave me her old beak plate. Puffins shed the bright outside of their beaks every fall, and grow a new one for spring.


🎉 I found the X!

There was an old wooden chest buried under it. Inside: a golden compass.

But here's the weird part. It doesn't point north. It points straight into the fog past the Tall Hills. 🤔

🗼 I lit OJ Light!

The fog wasn't hiding a monster. It was hiding the SEA. And on a rocky point over the waves, a lighthouse with a dark lamp.

The golden compass fit right into the clockwork under the lens, like a key. Click. Now the lens turns, and the light flashes two times every ten seconds. Bawk-bawk. That's its very own pattern, so ships know which lighthouse it is.

Then the compass popped back out. The needle swung around... and now it points home. 🏡

And way out there, past the point, I can see a little island. Hmm.
